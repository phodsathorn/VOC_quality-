Standard Operating Procedure

Protocol: Chemical Solvent Leak Response

Level 2 Hazard
1

Evacuate Booth

Immediately halt spray operations and evacuate personnel from Coating Booth 1 to the designated muster point.

2

Engage Emergency Ventilation

Switch exhaust damper to purge mode (100% stack extraction) via the booth override switch or Kiosk panel.

3

Isolate Solvents

Close manual shutoff valves on bulk solvent supply lines and lock paint feed pressure pots.

4

Notify Incident Commander

Verify automated LINE alert delivery and notify the EHS response team before re-entry.

Deployment Checklist

Powering and Provisioning E-Nose Node

Follow these sequential procedures to initialize a newly deployed telemetry enclosure on the powder coating line.

1

Mount Enclosure

Clip securely to TS-35 DIN rail at 1.5m height away from direct overspray jets.

2

Power 12VDC Line

Connect fused 12V supply through PG7 gland. Confirm green internal 3.3V LED illuminates.

3

Sensor Burn-in

Allow 24-hour continuous baseline burn-in before calibrating alarm thresholds.
